<objective>
Introduce the `Tab` enum (String raw values) and restructure the 5-tab navigation so that the tab bar order matches v2.0: Home, Goals, Explore, Community, Profile (Community and Explore swap positions; "Me" renamed to "Profile"). Migrate `ContentView`, `VGTabBar`, and `CommunityTabView` from `Int`-typed tab selection to the typed `Tab` enum across all selection bindings and `.tag()` values. Replace the hard-coded `selectedTab = 3` integer in `CommunityTabView` with `selectedTab = .explore`. Remove `.stats` and `.wins` from the Goals tab `navigationDestination` switch (rewiring on the Home tab happens in Plan 02). Provide minimal static placeholder views for the new Explore and Community tab slots so the app does not crash before Phases 20 and 21 fill them in.

Purpose: Establish the routing foundation for the entire v2.0 milestone. The Tab enum (TAB-04) protects against deep link and widget intent regressions caused by shifting tab indices. The reordered tab bar (TAB-01) is the visible v2.0 navigation shape.

Output: New `Tab.swift` and two placeholder view files; updated `ContentView`, `VGTabBar`, `CommunityTabView` with `Tab`-typed bindings, swapped Community/Explore positions, "Profile" label, and `.stats`/`.wins` removed from the Goals tab destinations.
</objective>
