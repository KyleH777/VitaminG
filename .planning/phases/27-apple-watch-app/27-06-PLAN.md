  <how-to-verify>
    **Required hardware:** an iPhone (iOS 17+) and a paired Apple Watch (watchOS 10+). The Simulator cannot test transferUserInfo per RESEARCH.md Pitfall 2.

    **Setup:**
    1. In Xcode, select VitaminG scheme. Choose your paired iPhone as the destination.
    2. Build & Run on iPhone (Cmd+R). After install completes, the Watch should auto-install VitaminGWatch via the iPhone's Watch app (or manually install it from the My Watch tab → Vitamin G → Install).
    3. On the Watch face, long-press → Edit → choose a watch face with rectangular complications (Modular, Infograph, etc.) → tap a rectangular slot → select Vitamin G → confirm.

    **Test A — WATCH-02 success criterion 1 (complication shows live data):**
    1. On iPhone, open Vitamin G. Confirm you have at least one active (non-completed) goal with a durationDays value set.
    2. Glance at the Watch face. Confirm the Vitamin G complication shows the active goal title and a progress ring matching the iPhone's current state (within 1–2 seconds after iPhone foreground).
    3. On iPhone, check in on the active goal (tap the check mark).
    4. Glance at the Watch face within 5 seconds — the complication should swap to the "Checked in" state (checkmark + active goal title in terraGlow color).

    **Test B — WATCH-03 success criterion 2 (Watch check-in updates iPhone):**
    1. On iPhone, ensure you have NOT checked in today on the active goal (delete the just-created CompletionEvent or reset via app uninstall+reinstall if needed).
    2. Note the iPhone's current global streak count on the Home tab.
    3. On the Watch, tap the Vitamin G complication to launch the app. TodayGlanceView appears with the Check In button enabled.
    4. Tap Check In on the Watch. The CheckInSuccessView glow-bloom animation plays immediately (optimistic UI per CONTEXT.md Claude's Discretion).
    5. Within 5 seconds, foreground the iPhone Vitamin G app.
    6. Confirm on the iPhone:
       - Home tab global streak incremented by 1 (or stayed the same if today was already a check-in day for some other goal — verify via the active goal's day grid)
       - The active goal shows today's check-in in its day grid
       - The home-screen iOS widget (if installed on the iPhone home screen) reflects the new streak/progress within ~10 seconds
    7. Re-glance at the Watch face — the complication now shows the "Checked in" state.
    8. Re-open the Watch app → TodayGlanceView Check In button is now disabled with "Checked in!" label.

    **Test C — WATCH-03 success criterion 3 (streak-at-risk cancellation):**
    1. On iPhone, ensure NotificationScheduler has scheduled the streak-at-risk one-shot for today (this requires the user to have a streak ≥ 3 days and not yet checked in by morning — or temporarily set `globalStreakAtRiskIdentifier` notification to fire in 1 minute via a debug build).
    2. Without checking in on iPhone, on the Watch open Vitamin G and tap Check In on TodayGlanceView.
    3. Wait for the next minute (or for the configured 7 PM trigger).
    4. Confirm no streak-at-risk notification fires on the iPhone OR on the Watch face. The single alert scheduled on iPhone was cancelled by the Watch check-in via the same `cancelGlobalStreakAtRiskNudge()` path.

    **Test D — graceful degradation (offline Watch):**
    1. On Watch, enable Airplane Mode (Settings → Airplane Mode).
    2. Tap Check In on TodayGlanceView. CheckInSuccessView appears optimistically.
    3. Disable Airplane Mode. Within 10–30 seconds (WCSession queue flush), confirm the iPhone receives the check-in (streak updates, widget refreshes). `transferUserInfo` is a queued delivery, so offline behavior must work per RESEARCH.md Pattern 1.
