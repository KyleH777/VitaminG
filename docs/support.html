Report a Bug

If you've found a bug, please include:

  • A brief description of the problem
  • Steps to reproduce it

  • Any screenshots that may help

Email bug reports to support@vitamingapp.com with the subject line "Bug Report".

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account and all associated data, email support@vitamingapp.com with the subject line "Delete My Account". We will process your request within 30 days.

How do I sign out?

Go to Settings inside the app and tap Sign Out.
