Privacy Policy

Vitamin G  ·  Last updated: July 3, 2026

Vitamin G ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive only the information Apple provides: a unique user identifier and, optionally, your name and email address. We never see your Apple ID password.

Photos you upload. Photos you choose to add within the app are stored securely and used solely to provide the features of Vitamin G. We do not use your photos for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond delivering the app's core functionality.

Community content. Goals you choose to make public, your username, profile motto, and community posts and reactions are visible to other Vitamin G users. Do not include information in public goals that you do not want others to see.

AI motivation features. When you use AI-powered motivation or goal suggestions, your goal titles, categories, and streak count are sent to our server and processed by Anthropic (our AI provider) to generate the response. No name, email, photos, or other account information is included in these requests. Responses are cached on your device.

Usage data. We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (e.g., crash reports) to improve app stability. This data cannot identify you personally.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To store and display content you create inside the app
  • To diagnose crashes and improve performance

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We share data only with the providers we rely on to run the app, and only to the extent needed:

  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit — storage and syncing of your goals and content across your devices
  • Cloudflare — request routing between the app and our server
  • Anthropic — AI text generation for motivation and goal suggestions, as described above
  • If required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app: Settings > Delete Account. Deletion removes your local data, synced iCloud data, and public community content. You may also email us and we will process the request within 30 days.

5. Payments

Vitamin G is free. Voluntary tips are processed entirely by Apple as in-app purchases; we never receive or store payment card details.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit and at rest) to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to safeguard your data.

7. Children's Privacy

Vitamin G is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

Questions or requests? Email us at VitaminG.info@gmail.com
