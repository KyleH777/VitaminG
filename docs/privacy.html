Privacy Policy

Vitamin G  ·  Last updated: June 10, 2026

Vitamin G ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive only the information Apple provides: a unique user identifier and, optionally, your name and email address. We never see your Apple ID password.

Content you create. Your goals, check-ins, streaks, and notes are stored locally on your device via SwiftData. If you enable iCloud sync, this data is stored in your personal iCloud account via CloudKit; we have no access to your private iCloud data.

Community content. When you choose to make a goal public, its title, category, and duration are visible to other users in the Explore feed. Private notes and daily check-in details are never made public.

AI-generated motivation. Vitamin G uses Anthropic's AI services to generate personalised motivational messages. To provide this feature, your goal titles, categories, and streak count are sent to Anthropic's servers. Your name, email address, and photos are never shared with Anthropic. AI-generated responses are cached on your device and are not retained by us beyond the current session.

Photos you upload. Photos you choose to add within the app are stored locally on your device (and optionally in your personal iCloud). We do not access, transmit, or use your photos for any purpose beyond displaying them within the app.

Usage data. We may collect anonymous, aggregated crash reports to improve app stability. This data cannot identify you personally.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To store and display content you create inside the app
  • To generate personalised motivational messages via AI
  • To diagnose crashes and improve performance

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We work with the following service providers to operate the app:

  • Apple (iCloud / CloudKit) — optional iCloud sync of your goals and check-ins, stored in your personal iCloud account under Apple's privacy terms.
  • Cloudflare — CDN and hosting for this website. Standard server access logs apply.
  • Anthropic — AI services used to generate motivational messages. Goal titles, categories, and streak count are sent; no personal identifiers are included. Data is processed under Anthropic's privacy terms.

We may also share data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly from within the app: go to Settings > Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible — all locally stored data is erased and any public content is removed from our servers.

You may also request deletion by emailing VitaminG.info@gmail.com. We will process email deletion requests within 30 days.

5. In-App Purchases & Payments

Vitamin G's core features are entirely free. If you choose to support development via the voluntary Tip Jar, payments are processed entirely by Apple through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We never receive, store, or have access to your payment card details or billing information.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit and at rest) to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to safeguard your data.

7. Children's Privacy

Vitamin G is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

Questions or requests? Email us at VitaminG.info@gmail.com
