Privacy Policy

Vitamin G  ·  Last updated: May 16, 2025

Vitamin G ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive only the information Apple provides: a unique user identifier and, optionally, your name and email address. We never see your Apple ID password.

Photos you upload. Photos you choose to add within the app are stored securely and used solely to provide the features of Vitamin G. We do not use your photos for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond delivering the app's core functionality.

Usage data. We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (e.g., crash reports) to improve app stability. This data cannot identify you personally.

2. How We Use Your Information

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share data only:

  • With service providers who help us operate the app (e.g., cloud storage), under strict confidentiality agreements

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

5. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit and at rest) to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to safeguard your data.

6. Children's Privacy

Vitamin G is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
