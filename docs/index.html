About

Built from the
hardest year
of my life.

K
Kyle Harrington
Founder & Developer

I was diagnosed with testicular cancer and given a new perspective on life overnight. At my worst, a mass the size of a softball was pressing against my heart and lungs. Breathing hurt. Moving hurt.

But I still made a goal every single day: walk the hospital halls at least once, and climb the stairs to the very top floor. Those small wins kept me grounded when everything else felt out of my control.

The day I rang the bell, my body proved it still had fight in it.

After that, I wanted to do things I simply could not do before. Running a 5k was one of them — not some big symbolic statement, just a goal that was out of reach when I was sick, and now it wasn't.

That experience taught me something I can't unlearn. Goals do not have to be big to matter. They just have to be yours. Gratitude works the same way — you find it in the small things, or you miss it entirely.

Vitamin G was built on that belief. Whether you are chasing a personal record or just trying to get out of bed and move today, this is a place to set your goal, show up, and appreciate how far you have already come.

Common questions

Good questions,
generous answers.

Is it really free? What's the catch?
No catch. Vitamin G is fully free, with no ads, no premium tier, no data sales, and no feature gating. If you'd like to support the project, the tip jar offers three optional one-time amounts — but the full app stays identical whether you give $0 or $500.
How do group goals stay encouraging instead of competitive?
Group challenges share a single collective ring rather than ranking individuals. Reactions are limited to positive ones — hearts, fire, claps. There are no comments threads, no public streak leaderboards, and you can toggle any individual goal private while still contributing to the group total.
What does the Apple Watch app do?
It's built for one-handed glances: see today's ring, tap to check in, watch the group ring update in real time. Complications surface your streak and group % on most watch faces. Morning notifications are gentle — never more than one per day, only at the time you choose.
Are my photos and check-ins private?
Every goal has a privacy toggle. Public check-ins go on your community's photo wall. Private check-ins still count toward your rings and the group's collective total — but no one else sees them. You can switch any check-in private after the fact.
How do streaks work? What if I miss a day?
Streaks build on consistency, not punishment. Every active user gets two free "streak freezes" per month — automatically applied on days you miss. We'll never send a guilt-trip notification about a broken streak, and you can disable streak counts entirely in settings.
What devices does Vitamin G support?
iPhone (iOS 16+) and Apple Watch (watchOS 10+). The iPhone app is the primary experience; the watch app is a glanceable companion. Android and web are on the longer-term roadmap but not imminent.
How do I delete my account or export my data?
Settings → Privacy → Export everything (you'll get a JSON file of all your goals, check-ins, and photos) or Delete account (instant, irreversible, processed within 24 hours). We don't make you email support to delete — that's not the deal.
What is the G in Vitamin G?
It means whatever you want it to mean. Goals? Sure. Growth? Absolutely. Gratitude? That's the whole point. Grit? On the hard days, yes. Getting out of bed? That counts too. The G is yours — we just made the bottle.
Does this condone drug use?
Absolutely not. Vitamin G is a vitamin — not a drug. Just like the vitamins you take for your health, this app is here to nourish your goals and well-being. We do not condone the use of drugs in any form. The name is about growth, gratitude, and showing up for yourself — nothing else.
